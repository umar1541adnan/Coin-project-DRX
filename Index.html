01 — About the project

What is DRX, and why I built it

A brand study in what makes a digital asset feel trustworthy.

DRX Trading Coin is a conceptual cryptocurrency brand created for a school portfolio assignment. The goal was to design a coin identity from the ground up — logo, color language, supply model, and a public-facing case for why a token like DRX might matter to traders — the same way a real project would pitch itself to investors.

02 — Tokenomics

How the 250M DRX supply is modeled

A fixed, non-inflationary supply split across four pools designed to balance early liquidity, long-term growth, and community access.

40%

Public & exchange liquidity

100,000,000 DRX reserved to seed trading pairs and market depth at launch.

25%

Ecosystem & partnerships

62,500,000 DRX allocated to integrations, grants, and platform incentives.

20%

Founding team

50,000,000 DRX under a multi-year vesting schedule to align long-term incentives.

15%

Community treasury

37,500,000 DRX reserved for holder governance proposals and rewards.

03 — Market thesis

The case for DRX's influence on the market

Four ideas explored in my research on how a token like DRX could theoretically shape trading behavior — presented here as an academic thesis, not a financial claim.

01 / Liquidity signaling

Deep pools build trust

Allocating 40% of supply to liquidity pools was modeled to reduce slippage and price volatility, a factor researchers link to healthier retail participation.

02 / Fixed supply narrative

Scarcity as a design choice

DRX's capped 250M supply mirrors the scarcity mechanics used by assets like Bitcoin, explored here as a case study in tokenized scarcity psychology.

03 / Governance access

Community treasury

The 15% community pool models how holder-driven proposals could decentralize decision-making rather than concentrating it with founders.

04 / Vesting discipline

Aligned incentives

Multi-year team vesting is a common real-world mechanism studied here to show how it discourages early sell-offs that can destabilize new markets.
